<h1>About</h1>

<p><strong>Aero/CFD Notes</strong> publishes one practical, technically-grounded tutorial per day covering CFD (ANSYS Fluent/CFX), finite element analysis, LS-DYNA, composite materials, and UAV/drone aerodynamics and structures.</p>

<p>The content pipeline is fully automated: trending topics in the aero/turbomachinery engineering space are queued, researched, and converted into step-by-step workflow articles with the common mistakes and checks a practitioner would actually perform. Every article is designed for engineering students and early-career engineers who need working procedure, not vague overviews.</p>

<p>Have a topic you'd like covered? It may already be queued — or browse the <a href="/">full article list</a>.</p>
